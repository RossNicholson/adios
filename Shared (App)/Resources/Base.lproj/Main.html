How to enable

  1. Open Safari
  2. Click Safari in the menu bar
  3. Click Settings…
  4. Click Extensions
  5. Enable Adios
  6. If Safari asks for permission, choose Always Allow on Every Website

Adios never collects, stores, or transmits any personal data. All blocking happens locally on your Mac.
