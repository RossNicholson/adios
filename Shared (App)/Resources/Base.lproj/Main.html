App installed! Now follow these steps:

  1. Close this app
  2. Open iPhone Settings
  3. Scroll down and tap Safari
  4. Tap Extensions
  5. Find Adios and turn it ON

When enabling the extension, Safari will ask for permission to access web content. This is required - Adios needs access to websites you visit to identify and block ads. While this means Adios can see the content of web pages, we never collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including passwords or credit card details.

To enable Adios:

  1. Open Safari
  2. Click Safari in the menu bar
  3. Click Settings...
  4. Click Extensions
  5. Find Adios and check the box to enable it

Safari will ask for permission to access all websites. This access is required - Adios needs to see web page content to identify and block ads effectively. While Adios can see the content of web pages, we never collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Your privacy is our priority.
