App installed! Now follow these steps to enable Adios:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap Apps
  3. Tap Safari
  4. Tap Extensions
  5. Find Adios in the list
  6. Toggle the switch to turn it ON
  7. When prompted, tap Allow to grant permission to access web content

Why does Adios need permission?
Adios needs access to web content to identify and block ads, trackers, and other unwanted content. When you enable the extension, Safari will ask for permission to access websites you visit.

Your privacy is protected: Adios never collects, stores, or transmits any personal information, including passwords, credit card details, or browsing history. All blocking happens locally on your device.

To enable Adios:

  1. Open Safari
  2. Click Safari in the menu bar
  3. Click Settings...
  4. Click Extensions
  5. Find Adios and check the box to enable it

Safari will ask for permission to access all websites. This access is required - Adios needs to see web page content to identify and block ads effectively. While Adios can see the content of web pages, we never collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Your privacy is our priority.
